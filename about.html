Girdharlal Tanna

Founder

Girdharlal Tanna

Founder

The visionary behind Durga Fire, Late Shri Girdharlal G. Tanna was the first Fire Fighting Superintendent in India's history to receive the Rashtrapati Award in 1975. His guiding philosophy - "We Serve To Save" - remains the soul of everything Durga Fire stands for today.

Harikrishna Tanna

Proprietor

Harikrishna Tanna

Proprietor

The architect of Durga Fire's growth, Shri Harikrishna G. Tanna has spent three decades building Gujarat's most trusted fire safety organization - growing it to 700+ professionals across 40+ industrial clients, making Durga Fire the gold standard across Gujarat's GIDC zones.

Yash Tanna

CEO

Yash Tanna

CEO

As the third generation to lead Durga Fire & Safety Consultants, Shri Yash H. Tanna brings fresh vision and modern leadership to a 35-year legacy. From managing fire operations at the iconic Statue of Unity to his mission of bringing fire safety awareness into every Indian home.

Our People

Our Team in Action

700+ safety professionals dedicated to protecting lives and critical industrial infrastructure.

Behind every deployment is a dedicated workforce of over 700+ fire safety officers, station commanders, and emergency response specialists. Driven by rigorous tactical discipline and continuous field training, our teams operate seamlessly across Gujarat's largest industrial hubs. Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Fire & Industrial Safety

Durga Fire & Safety Consultants is a trusted name in fire protection, industrial safety, emergency preparedness, and fire manpower services.

Founded on a legacy of commitment to public safety and industrial protection, we have spent over 35 years helping organizations strengthen their emergency response capabilities and maintain safe working environments.

Today, our team of 700+ trained professionals supports industries, government organizations, commercial establishments, hospitals, and institutions across Gujarat.
